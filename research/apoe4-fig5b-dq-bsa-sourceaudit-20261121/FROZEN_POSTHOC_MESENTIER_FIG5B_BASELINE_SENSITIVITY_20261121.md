 3) Raw 23h endpoint, 0h baseline, 23h minus 0h increase per replicate.

 5) Exact 2-sided UNPAIRED label permutation p for difference in means (4 vs4, 70 reallocations) on raw AUC and baseline-corrected AUC for MBCD and 2HBCD versus E4 control; source n4 is 4 experimental replicates not 4 independent donors. No p on every time point.
 6) Dose/intensity normalization: treatment/control mean ratios descriptively only, not mechanistic uptake correction. Baseline(t0) is DQ-BSA assay fluorescence, NOT direct measured cargo uptake; subtracting t0 is NOT a calibrated dextran uptake correction.
 7) Technical checks: each trajectory 24 timestamps, no missing/negative/nonfinite raw intensities, no repeated t, source SHA exact; quantify coefficient of variation across replicates and whether a single run dominates the means.
 8) Additional optional check comparisons vs paper mention FigS4e independent dextran-normalized baseline genotype result. Do NOT claim Fig5b had dextran-normalized rescue unless original source includes matching dextran values or author paper explicit method for same experiments.

INFERENCE GATE: If MBCD treatment shows higher raw AUC but loses effect after baseline subtraction, only claim raw fluorescence improvement with pronounced initial baseline confounding. If baseline-subtracted effect remains, claim treatment-linked rise in DQ-BSA FLUORESCENCE dynamics, NOT proven clearance per internalized cargo.
