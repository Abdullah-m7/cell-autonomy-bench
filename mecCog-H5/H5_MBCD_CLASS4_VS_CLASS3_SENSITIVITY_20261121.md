# H5 MβCD: conservative Class 4 / d4 directness sensitivity (NOT SUBMITTED)
**Mechanism Atlas | 21 November 2026 | Science reviewer sensitivity, NOT a new experimental finding.**

The H5 hypothesis: In some types of non-Alzheimer's, non-aged human brain cells, APOE4 disruption of lipid metabolism, compared with APOE3, results in impaired late endosome/lysosome function.

**Official MecCog 2026 directness rubric:** https://meccog-meccog-challenge.static.hf.space/rules.html — Class 3: direct perturbation/variation of causal A with surrogate B-prime readout; Class 4: perturbation of surrogate A-prime and measurement of surrogate B-prime; strength d4 weak when a known alternative mechanism affects the source surrogate reporter. No fixed numerical ranking formula is published. This rating requires human science adjudication.

**Source:** Mesentier-Louro et al. Cell Stem Cell 2026 DOI https://doi.org/10.1016/j.stem.2026.08.001. Human isogenic APOE4 iPSC astrocytes. MβCD treatment alters cholesterol availability (an A-prime surrogate relative to broad APOE4-disrupted lipid metabolism A); DQ-BSA cleavage-proxy integrated fluorescence is a B-prime surrogate relative to actual late lysosomal enzymatic degradation B. Treatment uptake, lysosome number/pH, fluorophore physicochemistry and true cleavage of controlled internalized peptide are not measured together in each source Figure 5b or Figure S5c assay.
