# Mechanism Atlas — MecCog Step 1 H3 evidence-extraction method (2026-11-20)

**Status:** Public source-audit method for an **unsubmitted draft**, not independent biological experimentation or a confirmed discovery. **Team:** Mechanism Atlas. **Who did the work:** both (researcher directed scientific priorities and source selection; GPT-6/ChatGPT assisted primary-source searching, evidence extraction, code, critical limitations and document authoring). No independent expert sign-off of every row is claimed.

## Exact H3 hypothesis

> In some types of non-Alzheimer's, non-aged human brain cells, APOE4 undergoes increased aggregation in the endo-lysosome compared with APOE3.

The full statement has **four conditions**: APOE4 vs APOE3, **protein aggregation** (not simply intracellular protein abundance/puncta or co-localization), **endo-lysosomal compartment**, and **non-Alzheimer's, non-aged human brain cells**. Most available assays prove only some conditions, so all H3 evidence is provisionally graded cautiously, often `Class 5 Other`, rather than presented as a direct human-brain-cell mechanism.

**Correction check completed:** Kaji et al. issued a 2025 erratum (DOI `10.1016/j.immuni.2024.11.024`) changing Figure 5C TREM2KO quantitative graph and relabeling Figure 7B from IBA1 and MX04 aggregates to LAMP1-positive, APOE+/Aβ42+ lysosomes. See [the audited corrigendum](ERRATUM_KAJI_2024.md). The draft P1.F1–P1.F5 do **not** transcribe uncorrected Figure 5C numbers or claim Fig7B is a genotype-specific aggregate quantification. Lysosomal dual-positive cargo must not be described as a direct fibrillar APOE aggregate count.

## Author-supplied Xia 2024 Fig 4 numerical audit — source-label NO-GO

The original publisher [author source Excel](https://static-content.springer.com/esm/art%3A10.1038%2Fs41467-024-49028-z/MediaObjects/41467_2024_49028_MOESM4_ESM.xlsx) was downloaded, SHA256 `47d9439f523f58c2e075e0dd853220da9b7971fa3541cfc675913c88db86617e`. It has 18 sheets. A source parser and reproducible posthoc calculation are in `mecCog-H3/audit_xia2024_fig4_source.py`. The `Fig. 4` sheet has three normalized culture values per condition (microglia rows 4–6, astrocytes rows 8–10), as the paper's Figure 4 legend states.

**Unambiguous source comparisons (row 2 header “Non-lipidated apoE”, row 3 condition label ApoE3 vs ApoE4 at t1)**:
- human iPSC microglia-like cells, ApoE3 values 2.59574/2.35268/2.81699, ApoE4 1.33511/1.10714/1.73203. Means 2.588470 vs 1.391427; E4/E3 ratio 0.53755 or 46.25% lower uptake.
- human astrocyte-like cells, ApoE3 0.70455/0.80086/0.78555, ApoE4 0.55785/0.58458/0.63636. Means 0.763653 vs 0.592930; E4/E3 ratio 0.77644 or 22.36% lower uptake.
- Exploratory unpaired exact culture-label permutations of three E3 versus three E4 readings yield **two-sided p=0.10 for each** (20 possible labels); this is **our posthoc sensitivity calculation**, NOT the authors' p-value, not adjusted for testing, and assumes exchangeability despite possible same-batch structure. Thus the spreadsheet leaves published p as `N/A` rather than falsely reporting our exploratory p as author statistics.

**Source ambiguity requiring suspension, not guesswork:** in the Fig 4 source workbook, the row-2 `Lipidated apoE` section has two t1 columns labelled `apoE3` and **no unique apoE4 t1 column** in row 3, alongside other duplicated t3 labels. These appear to be source-label discrepancies, but a putative ApoE4 column was **NOT assigned or used** in our numeric genotype/isoform comparison. Consequently the script emits `NO_GO` for the lipidated E3-vs-E4 numeric result pending published legend/author clarification. The paper's original statement that lipidation improves coaggregate uptake is separate evidence, not license to fabricate a numeric ApoE4 source mapping.

**Key mechanistic separation:** in this study differences in **entry/clearance** of preformed mixed ApoE–Aβ coaggregates should not be misnamed differences in ApoE4 **de novo aggregation** in lysosomes. This distinction is critical for MecCog's highly qualified H3 statement.

## Xia Figure 7 HAE-4 intervention: a second causal interpretation survives (21 November 2026)

This is **author-published 2024 data**, reanalyzed after inspecting the paper, **not a new intervention or blinded validation**. Source spreadsheet `41467_2024_49028_MOESM4_ESM.xlsx`, SHA256 `47d9439f523f58c2e075e0dd853220da9b7971fa3541cfc675913c88db86617e`, original Fig 7 C–L, 3 source biological replicates per comparison. The HAE-4 antibody **immunodepletes extracellular early nonlipidated ApoE4–Aβ coaggregates before cell exposure**; it is **not an intracellular or surface-receptor inhibitor**.

Relative to isotype immunoprecipitation control, HAE-4 removal reduced fluorescent cargo uptake and secreted cytokines, respectively:

| Recipient culture | Uptake decrease | MCP-1 decrease | IL-1β decrease | IL-6 decrease | TNF-α decrease |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Human iPSC microglia-like | 32.2% | 19.6% | 23.2% | 20.2% | 28.7% |
| Human astrocyte-like | 44.4% | 17.4% | 22.6% | 21.5% | 18.5% |

All ten group contrasts are descriptive source-value ratios. A posthoc exact unpaired 3-versus-3 label permutation has minimum two-sided p=0.10, which is what the audit returned; **do not present this as the authors' published test**.

**Crucial red-team test:** A decrease in cytokine output **smaller** than the decrease in uptake for 8/8 markers might seem to imply a surface-proximal inflammatory signal. We tested the less exciting explanation first. For each marker/cell model, an affine uptake-proxy-only relation `Y = a + b U` was fitted to the two group means (isotype, HAE-4) and then used to predict the third unprocessed ApoE4–Aβ source arm. The **median absolute relative prediction error was 3.44%**, max **6.53%**. **7/8** predictions differed from their original unprocessed-arm mean by no more than **one source culture SD**, and all fitted intercepts and slopes were nonnegative. This means this small three-arm dataset **does not require invoking an additional surface-sensing mechanism**; it does **not prove** uptake causes the cytokines. The fit used two points for two parameters (zero model-fitting degrees of freedom), with only one third-arm group-mean check. Data are unpaired across assay types and ligand composition changes after HAE-4 depletion, preventing causal mediation or instrumental-variable analysis.

Scripts (original source data downloaded separately from publisher, not committed to repo):
- `mecCog-H3/audit_xia2024_HAE4_immunodepletion.py` — verifies exact author source checksum, group labels, 10 isotype comparisons, original source triplicate records and descriptive p.
- `mecCog-H3/run_hae4_three_arm_uptake_model.py` — checks whether original untreated mixture cytokine means are predictable from the two HAE-4/isotype mean points.

**Necessary experiment to discriminate:** alter cell-surface ligand contact independently of internalized cargo and aggregate composition using calibrated extracellular tethering/noninternalizable presentation plus separate orthogonal uptake manipulation, while measuring early NF-κB/inflammasome signaling, receptor-specific binding, uptake-adjusted lysosomal degradation and toxicity **in the same isogenic human genetic backgrounds**. Surface and intracellular contributions may coexist. This experiment has NOT been executed. The Nobel-level causal mechanism remains a research ambition, not a discovery. **No MecCog workbook row was added based solely on these posthoc ratios or model coefficients.**

## Separate Aβ fibrillation kinetic reanalysis (21 November 2026; not direct H3 evidence)

The original publisher author XLSX `41467_2024_49028_MOESM4_ESM.xlsx` (SHA256 `47d9439f523f58c2e075e0dd853220da9b7971fa3541cfc675913c88db86617e`) includes **241 serial Thioflavin-T fluorescence points per condition** in sheet `Figure 1A`. Source-locked posthoc analysis `mecCog-H3/audit_xia2024_ThT_kinetics.py` calculated dynamic fluorescence time-to-half-maximum using median early baseline and late plateau, sustained threshold crossings and time interpolation (alternative baseline/plateau sensitivity also run).

| Cell-free Aβ42 reaction | t50 (minutes) | Comparator |
|---|---:|---|
| Aβ42 alone | 26.29 | — |
| Aβ42 + **lipidated ApoE3** | 18.93 | — |
| Aβ42 + **lipidated ApoE4** | 22.77 | E4–E3 **+3.84 min** |
| Aβ42 + **nonlipidated ApoE3** | 47.29 | — |
| Aβ42 + **nonlipidated ApoE4** | 45.58 | E4–E3 **−1.71 min** |

The opposite sign of the E4–E3 timing difference under different lipidation conditions was retained with an alternative 20%-terminal/8-point-baseline definition (≈+3.81 and −1.53 minutes). This is **descriptive reanalysis of the authors' original extracellular Aβ fibrillization measurements**, not a new biological experiment. Figure 1A in the paper reports n=3 independent experiments but the provided worksheet has **one aggregated trace per condition**; **the 241 clock-time measurements are not 241 independent replicates**, so no p-value/CI is derived. Most importantly, **ThT is a probe of Aβ fibrillation, NOT de novo APOE4 endolysosomal aggregation**; this does not justify reclassifying H3 findings as direct. It motivates measuring both biological *aggregation maturity* and *clock time* when comparing isoform effects on glial cargo uptake and cytokines, ideally in matched donor/cargo conditions.

## Official template and coding

- File uses **19 columns** in the challenge's exact order; P1–P5 paper rows and sequential P#.F# rows. Preserve full exact H3 statement in cell A2.
- For each finding, specify the comparator, species, age/pathology, APOE lipidation, measured soluble/insoluble biochemical fraction or dye behavior, compartment marker, figure panel, source of effect size/p/n and whether any counts are independent donor/animal/culture units.
- Only directly published numerical values are put into Effect size `40%`, `2.5x`, or descriptive `qualitative`; when a percent or fold number is computed by us rather than printed, describe its provenance honestly and do not create a fabricated author p-value. Missing p/n are `N/A` with `not_found_in_source`.
- Do not call antibody intensity, diffraction-limited puncta, colocalization, or CSF insoluble fraction *proof of fibrillar aggregates inside human brain-cell lysosomes*. Multiple independent orthogonal readouts are needed.
- The official rubric defines Class 1 direct A→B, 2 indirect input, 3 indirect output, 4 both indirect, 5 Other. A figure about APOE4 versus APOE3 in purified solution but **without endolysosomes** is still **Class 5 Other** for the exact H3 statement. Its relevance may be high despite weak directness.
- Authors' sample-level n must not be inflated by images, microscopy fields, cells, mice in other panels, or nonmatching genotype groups.

The source bibliography and figure map are frozen in `mecCog-H3/source_manifest.json`. ### Original mechanistic hypothesis (UNPROVEN)

ApoE4 lipidation and endosomal membrane composition may jointly determine when **conformational aggregation actually initiates** in living human non-AD glia under matched extracellular APOE loading; this cannot be inferred from combining purified in vitro ApoE aggregation and endolysosomal staining in AD mice. A causal study would require matched human isogenic APOE3/4 iPSC astrocytes/microglia from multiple parental donor backgrounds, calibrated endolysosomal pH/lipidation, and orthogonal aggregate-specific assays (FLIM-FRET plus insoluble fractionation/structural imaging) with reversible rescue and independent culture replication. Measure entry and clearance separately; lipidation rescue alone has been reported previously.
