# Source correction audit — Kaji et al. Immunity 2024 (verified 2026-11-20)

Original article: Kaji et al., *Immunity* 57:2651–2668.e12 (2024), DOI `10.1016/j.immuni.2024.09.014`, PMID `39419029`.

**Published corrigendum:** Kaji et al., *Immunity* 58:263 (14 January 2025), DOI `10.1016/j.immuni.2024.11.024`, PMID `39637851`. Corrections described in the Technical University of Munich's full erratum abstract:
- **Figure 5C, right bar graph:** previously used incorrect underlying numbers for MX04-positive aggregates in TREM2-knockout cells. The bar graph was replaced. Statistical test description changed to one-way ANOVA/Tukey for the left graph and Student's t test for the right. Authors state the correction does not change their study's conclusions.
- **Figure 7B legend:** labeling `IBA1+ (magenta)` was corrected to `LAMP1+ (magenta)`; outcome `numbers of MX04+ aggregates` was corrected to `numbers of APOE+ and Aβ-42+ lysosomes`.

**Consequences for MecCog H3 evidence quality:** an `APOE+ and Aβ-42+ lysosome` count is **not** a count of verified molecularly aggregated APOE fibrils, let alone an isoform contrast in healthy human microglia. Do not reconstruct Kaji Fig 5C statistics from the uncorrected original PDF. Keep any future use of Fig 7B tied to **LAMP1+ organelles and APOE/Aβ dual positive lysosomes**, not `IBA1+` microglia or `MX04+` fibril numbers.

Our drafted P1.F1–F5 cite Kaji Fig 3D-G/S4 (purified lipidated/unlipidated APOE), Figure 4A–B (disease-model mouse microglia), Figure 5A–B/S6 (cell-culture aggregate context). They **do not claim numerical values from the corrected Figure 5C or mislabeled Figure 7B**. The erratum therefore does not require inventing a changed quantitative effect for these five rows; it *does* make the limited assay/compartment interpretation even more important. Check the original paper PDF and relevant primary figure legends before final competition submission.
