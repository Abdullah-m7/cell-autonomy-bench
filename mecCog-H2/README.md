# Mechanism Atlas — MecCog Step 1 H2 evidence method (20 November 2026)

**Status:** Public source methodology; H2 work-in-progress workbook is **not an official submission** or a scientific discovery. **Team:** Mechanism Atlas; researcher-led paper selection and critical evaluation, GPT-6 (ChatGPT) assisted figure provenance, computation, writing and source audits. The project did not receive independent expert signoff for every row.

## Exact challenge hypothesis

> In some types of non-Alzheimer's, non-aged human brain cells, for example astrocytes, APOE4 causes impaired ARF6-related recycling of receptors between the cell surface and the endosome, compared with APOE3.

This statement has **three distinct requirements**: APOE3/E4 comparison, a **receptor's surface/endosome recycling**, and an **ARF6-related** route. Endocytosis, total ARF6 protein, ARF6-GTP activity, Rab11 colocalization, receptor surface abundance, and the true return-to-surface recycling rate are separate observations. None should be silently treated as interchangeable.

**Critical distinction:** ABCA1 (the transporter in the organizer's starting reference) is an ATP-binding cassette **lipid transporter**, **not a conventional ligand receptor**. Rawat's primary paper is biologically relevant to ARF6 and endosomal routing, yet none of its ABCA1 experiments alone proves the exact full H2 claim about recycling *receptors* in non-aged human brain cells. We retain its original experiments while grading the full hypothesis conservatively.

## Posthoc exact culture-label robustness audit (source-only)

The new published script `mecCog-H2/run_pxd_ARF6_RAB7A_culture_audit.py` checks the author's source-derived five APOE3 / five APOE4 iAstrocyte abundance values, recomputes group mean log2FC, enumerates all **252** unpaired 5-of-10 genotype-label allocations, bootstraps 20,000 within-culture draws, and assesses 25 one-culture-drop pairs. Running against the saved author-processed data yielded ARF6 `+0.558058978` (exact permutation p `0.0079365`, culture bootstrap 95% `[+0.453,+0.671]`) and RAB7A `−0.776614432` (same exact permutation p, bootstrap `[−0.872,−0.675]`). Both retain effect sign in the 25 leave-one-of-each-genotype combinations. This analysis was chosen **after** reading the author's differential-abundance findings, is **not** a blinded replication, and has **one isogenic parental background**; the 5 culture replicates are not 5 unrelated donors. This exploratory p is **not** the authors' FDR, and total ARF6 abundance is **not** an ARF6-GTP assay.

An independent source-audit script on the researcher's Mac, `h2_recycling_20261120/audit_h2_original_figures.py`, verified figure legends and HTML source hashes for Rawat 2019 (Figs 1,2,3,5,6,7) and Mukhamedova 2016 (Figs 4,5). Narayan 2020 Fig1/4 was confirmed from the published PMC results, but the automated archive retrieval for its full figure bundle timed out; Bell Figure5/6 source was confirmed previously in the separate H1 provenance run. Do not claim the source automation exhaustively verified all figures.

Additional prior art: Dutta & Donaldson 2015 DOI `10.1111/tra.12302` showed Rab35 recruitment of ACAP ARF6-GAPs to rescue CD147 recycling tubules under clathrin perturbation. Francis et al. 2022 DOI `10.1038/s41467-022-32853-5` reported elevated ARF6-GTP upon Rab35 depletion in endothelial cells, but constitutively active ARF6 failed to phenocopy the main Rab35-dependent endothelial sprouting defects. A new proposed APOE4 human-glial local ARF6 activity gate **must demonstrate causal relevance beyond these already published mechanisms**, with direct ARF6-GTP and receptor surface-return kinetics in the same matched donor/cargo system. Neither published experiment by itself satisfies MecCog H2's human APOE genotype-specific causal criterion.

## Extraction and numerical provenance

1. Check official hypothesis at the [MecCog hypotheses page](https://meccog-meccog-challenge.static.hf.space/hypotheses.html) and the [19-column scoring specification](https://meccog-meccog-challenge.static.hf.space/rules.html). An experiment gets **Class 5 Other** when it does not instantiate the full A→B hypothesis; explain why and retain negative/indirect findings rather than inflating scores.
2. For each study identify original figure panel, experimental cell species and background, comparator, endpoint, p and n exactly as stated. `n=50–70 cells` is NOT independent donor n. When only star symbols with multiple treatments are given, leave per-pair p **N/A** rather than guessing.
3. Read Rawat figures 1–7 at PMC6880458. **ABCA1 = transporter**; membrane ABCA1 abundance/colocalization are proxies rather than receptor recycling rates. Rawat's total ARF6 immunoblot is NOT an ARF6-GTP pulldown.
4. Read Mukhamedova 2016 figures 1,4,5: keep cholesterol state-specific nulls. No APOE genotype manipulation; negative or off-context experiments are **contextual**.
5. Read Bell Figure 6B,D normalized pulse–chase **rate** vs Figure 6A,C absolute amount. Higher total uptake is not an increased k_in or decreased k_rec.
6. For Lysaker PXD077344, the original public author-processed XML includes `logFC_APOE4_A_vs_APOE3_A`, adjusted p-values and five culture-level abundance values per genotype. The shipped `reproduce_pxd_ARF6.py` (adapted from the project source parser) reads that file plus `WilkinsHM_APOE_Brain_BrainMito_iNeurons_iAstrocytes_Metadata.xlsx` locally; this repository does not redistribute those source datasets. Source genes ARF6, RAB7A are **whole-cell proteins**, not active forms. The processed logFC is transcribed as a source quantity; don't mistake sample counts for independent donors.
7. Reference the public Method URL and **pinned git commit SHA** in the Excel Method tab. Use `printed_in_text`, `supplement_only`, and `not_found_in_source` literally as defined by the challenge. Never synthesize a paper p-value or human drug-response claim.

## Additional directness gate: true human Tf recycling null (21 November 2026)

The original 5-paper / 20-finding H2 spreadsheet conservatively labels all findings Class5. A separate **unsubmitted scientific-sensitivity candidate** proposes **Class3/d4 weak** for **P4.F1** and **P4.F2** from Bell et al., *Stem Cell Reports* 2025 DOI [10.1016/j.stemcr.2025.102607](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12447338/).

In human isogenic APOE3/E4 iPSC-derived brain endothelial cells, Figure 5H shows **fewer Tf-positive endosomal sorting tubules** with E4, but Figure 6B/D finds **similar normalized transferrin pulse–chase recycling rates** across both independent parental genetic backgrounds; initial total Tf loading is higher under E4. These are different observables, and the normalized recycling result is explicit source **negative evidence**. The paper did **not** measure ARF6-GTP in these cells: the complete H2 claim about *ARF6-related* recycling remains unproven, which is why even these relatively relevant findings are not labeled Class1. A referee may reasonably retain the original Class5 rather than provisional Class3 when assigning evidence to the full pathway. **P4.F3** (endocytosis uptake) remains Class5; all other 18 results remain untouched.

Read the [Bell figure-level source adjudication](BELL2025_TFR_RECYCLING_NULL_DIRECTNESS_SENSITIVITY_20261121.md) before interpreting this as final. No official challenge submission or biological discovery occurred.

## Explicit scientific boundary and possible falsifier

These experiments suggest at least **two non-equivalent routes**: ABCA1 transporter handling may change with APOE4 and ARF6 abundance, while **TfR1's true normalized recycling rate can remain unchanged** in a human endothelial context; early uptake is PICALM sensitive; the outcome of ARF6 silencing varies with cholesterol loading. That is a **hypothesis-generating synthesis of existing studies**, not a new proven biochemical discovery.

An original experiment would require multiple genetically distinct, human, isogenic APOE3/4 parental backgrounds, matched cell/cargo assays with *direct* ARF6-GTP and receptor-return kinetics, ARF6 separation-of-function rescues, measured lipid/compartment pH, and a prespecified genotype×cargo×ARF6 perturbation contrast. No wet-lab data for this program have been obtained.
